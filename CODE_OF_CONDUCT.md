# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and
respectful experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

Be kind, constructive, and respectful. Focus criticism on the work, assume
good intent, and respect differing viewpoints.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and publishing private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository maintainers. Reports
will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially.
